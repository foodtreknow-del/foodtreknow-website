FoodTrekNow Legal

Privacy Policy

Effective: August 27, 2026

This policy explains how FoodTrekNow, operated by Kevin Lisenby ("FoodTrekNow," "we," "us," or "our"), handles information when customers order from participating food trucks and vendors operate their businesses through our website and mobile applications.

1. Scope

This policy applies to FoodTrekNow websites, mobile applications, customer ordering features, vendor tools, and related support services. Participating food trucks are independent businesses. A truck may have its own privacy practices for information it receives to prepare and fulfill an order.

3. How we use information

  • Create, authenticate, and maintain customer and vendor accounts.
  • Use a customer's chosen or current location to calculate nearby-truck distances and use vendor-shared GPS updates to display the truck's live business location and provide directions.
  • Show nearby or selected food trucks, process orders, coordinate pickup, and provide order status updates.
  • Send order information to the selected food truck and support messages between the customer and vendor.
  • Facilitate vendor payments, customer refunds, vendor-specific credits, and vendor subscriptions through Stripe.
  • Remember preferences, addresses, favorites, order history, notification settings, and privacy choices.
  • Review vendor applications, prevent fraud and abuse, secure the service, troubleshoot errors, and enforce our Terms.
  • Send promotions or favorite-truck notifications when you enable those preferences. You may turn them off in Settings.
  • Meet legal, tax, accounting, dispute, and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not use personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. How we disclose information

We disclose information only as reasonably needed for the purposes described above:

  • Selected food truck vendors: order items, instructions, pickup contact details, payment status, messages, and other information needed to fulfill or resolve an order.
  • Service providers: Supabase provides authentication, database, storage, and backend infrastructure; Stripe provides payment, refund, subscription, identity, and vendor payout services; hosting and app-store providers help deliver the service.
  • Legal and safety purposes: when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, vendors, FoodTrekNow, or the public.
  • Business changes: as part of a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or transfer of all or part of the service, subject to appropriate protections.

Service providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We require service providers to handle information consistently with their role and applicable law.

5. Payments and financial information

Customer payments are processed through the selected vendor's Stripe-connected account. FoodTrekNow facilitates the transaction but does not collect customer order proceeds for itself. Stripe collects and processes payment credentials. Vendors also use Stripe for onboarding and payouts.

FoodTrekNow charges participating vendors a platform subscription after any applicable free trial. We receive subscription status and limited billing information from Stripe but do not receive complete card numbers or card security codes.

6. Retention and security

We retain information while an account is active and as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain transaction records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, enforce agreements, and meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements. Retention periods vary by record type. Account deletion removes or de-identifies information that is not required to be retained. Some records may remain in backups or legally required transaction records for a limited period.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, including encrypted network connections, access controls, and provider security features. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your privacy choices

  • Access and correction: review and edit profile information, addresses, preferences, and notification settings from your account.
  • Customer location: use a manually selected city or ZIP instead of GPS, and revoke FoodTrekNow location permission through device settings. A customer GPS position is saved only in that customer's local app state for nearby searches.
  • Vendor live location: location sharing is off by default. An authorized vendor must tap Start Sharing Location and can tap Stop Sharing at any time. Public access to a live update expires when the update becomes stale.
  • Marketing and notifications: disable promotions, favorite-truck alerts, and push preferences in Settings. Essential order and account messages may still be sent.
  • Delete account: use Customer Account → Settings → Delete My Account. Vendors may contact us to request account deletion after resolving required transaction or subscription records.
  • Consent: withdraw optional permissions through account or device settings. Withdrawal does not affect processing already completed.

To request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of information that cannot be managed in the app, email kevinl@foodtreknow.com. We may verify your identity before completing a request.

8. Children

FoodTrekNow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it when required.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service, providers, or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when a material change requires it. Continued use after an update is subject to the revised policy.
